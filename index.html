USER-FIRST METHODOLOGY

How I turn messy problems into effortless experiences.

My 4-stage framework bridges business objectives, human psychology, and production-ready developer handoff.

PHASE 01

User Empathy & Research

Understanding who uses the product and why. Persona mapping, journey mapping, task analysis, and uncovering the root friction points.

PHASE 02

Architecture & Logic

Structuring the cognitive pathway. Low-fidelity wireframes, information hierarchies, click-through flows, and validating mental models early.

PHASE 03

Visual Craft & Tokens

High-fidelity UI, typography scale, micro-interactions, Auto Layout 5.0, dark/light variants, and responsive viewport considerations.

PHASE 04

SSOT & Dev Handoff

Single Source of Truth tokens mapped to Tailwind / React, detailed component states (hover, active, disabled), and developer walkthroughs.
